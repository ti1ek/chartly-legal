Chartly is a macOS utility. You select a region of your own screen that shows a price chart; the App reads that image, identifies candles and common chart formations in it, and draws an annotated copy of the picture. 4. Accuracy — what the App can and cannot do

The App reads a screenshot. That means its output depends entirely on what your screen happened to show and on how well the image could be interpreted. It may:

  • misread candles, wicks, colours or axes, particularly on unusual chart themes, low-contrast images, small crops, or images containing indicators, drawings or overlapping windows;
  • identify a formation that a human analyst would not identify, or miss one that a human analyst would;
  • mark a level or a formation that has already lost its relevance;
  • produce different results for the same chart at different times, because part of the analysis is performed by a statistical model whose output is not deterministic.

The App reads no price axis and therefore states no prices; positions it marks are positions in an image. You are solely responsible for verifying anything the App draws against your own charts and your own analysis before relying on it for any purpose.
